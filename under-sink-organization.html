Under-Sink Organization: Work Around the Pipes, Not Against Them

Organization · Updated October 27, 2026

The under-sink cabinet is awkward on purpose: pipes, a disposal and sometimes a filter take the middle. Organizers that ignore the plumbing end up wedged in or unused.

Measure three things first

  1. Interior width and depth of the cabinet.
  2. Height under the lowest pipe or drain trap.
  3. The width of the clear zones on either side of the plumbing.

Write them down before shopping; organizer listings give exact dimensions.

Pull-out tiers for one side

A two-tier pull-out unit can live beside the pipes and bring bottles at the back within reach. Check the stated dimensions against your clear zone.

Safety first

  • Keep products in their original, labeled containers and follow each product label.
  • If young children visit, use a cabinet lock or move cleaners up high.
  • Check for slow leaks while the cabinet is empty; a dry tray or towel makes drips easy to spot.

FAQ

What is the best way to organize under a kitchen sink?
Measure around the plumbing, use a pull-out or tiered organizer on one side, and contain small items in a removable bin.
Can I use adhesive hooks inside a cabinet door?
Command says its hooks are for smooth, finished indoor surfaces; check the door material and weight limit, and follow the mounting instructions.
