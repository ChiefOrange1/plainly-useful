Holiday Hosting Prep Checklist: A Week-by-Week Plan for Small Kitchens

Hosting · Updated October 27, 2026

Hosting in a small kitchen is a logistics problem: oven time, fridge space and dishes. This checklist spreads the work across two weeks.

Two weeks out

  • Lock the menu and guest count; ask about allergies.
  • Map oven time: list each dish, its temperature and how long it takes.
  • Count serving dishes, utensils and chairs; borrow early.

One week out

  • Clear fridge space: use up leftovers and condiments (see our fridge guide).
  • Shop for non-perishables.
  • Make-ahead items that freeze well go in labeled containers.

Day before

  • Set the table.
  • Prep vegetables and store them covered in the fridge.
  • Set up a coffee station so guests can serve themselves.

FAQ

How long can holiday food sit out?
FDA guidance is no more than 2 hours, or 1 hour if the air is above 90°F.
How do I host in a small kitchen?
Map oven time ahead, clear fridge space the week before, and use sheet pans and make-ahead dishes.
