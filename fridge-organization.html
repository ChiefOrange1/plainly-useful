Fridge organization is half tidiness and half food safety. Start with the temperature, then give every category a zone.

Check the temperature

The FDA advises keeping the refrigerator at or below 40°F (4°C) and the freezer at 0°F (-18°C), and using an appliance thermometer to check. It also advises not packing the fridge so tightly that air can't circulate.

A simple zone map

  • Eye level: leftovers and ready-to-eat food (an 'eat first' bin helps).
  • Lower shelf: raw meat in a tray or bin so drips can't reach other food.
  • Drawers: produce.
  • Door: condiments.

Bins and a turntable

Clear bins turn deep shelves into pull-out drawers; a turntable keeps jars from hiding in the back corner.

FAQ

What temperature should my fridge be?
FDA guidance is 40°F (4°C) or below for the refrigerator and 0°F (-18°C) for the freezer.
How do I stop forgetting leftovers?
Keep an 'eat first' bin at eye level and use clear containers.
