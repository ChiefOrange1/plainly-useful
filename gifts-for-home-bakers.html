Gifts for Home Bakers: Practical Tools, Explained by Spec (Not Hype)

Kitchen gifts · Updated October 27, 2026

Most home bakers already own the basics. The best baking gifts tend to be upgrades to the tools they reach for every time: the sheet pan, the measuring cups and something for zest and spice. Here is what to look at and why.

1. A sturdy half sheet with a rack

A half sheet is the standard home-oven size. A matching rack lets cookies cool with air underneath and lets roasts or wings cook raised off the pan. Check oven-safe temperature and care: uncoated aluminum is typically hand-wash only.

FAQ

What size sheet pan fits a standard home oven?
A half sheet is the common home size. The Nordic Ware half sheet in this guide is listed at about 17.9 x 12.9 in; measure your oven before buying.
Are aluminum baking sheets dishwasher safe?
Nordic Ware recommends hand washing its uncoated aluminum sheets because dishwashers discolor them.
