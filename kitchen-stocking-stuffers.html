Small organizing fixes

Adhesive hooks are a renter-friendly way to give an oven mitt or apron a home. Check the surface and weight limits first.

Command Large Utility Hook

Command (3M)

Best for: hanging oven mitts, aprons or a dish brush without drilling

  • Holds up to 5 lb, per 3M
  • For smooth, finished indoor surfaces such as painted walls, finished wood, glass, tile and metal; not for wallpaper
  • 3M says to wait one hour after mounting before hanging anything
  • Designed to remove without holes, marks or sticky residue when the instructions are followed

Edible and DIY fillers

  • Whole nutmeg (pairs with a grater).
  • Loose-leaf tea or local coffee beans.
  • A hand-written 'family recipe' card.
  • Paper filters for the coffee brewer they already own.

FAQ

What is a practical kitchen stocking stuffer?
A small tool used weekly, such as a fine grater or a nesting measuring set, or a consumable like spices or coffee.
