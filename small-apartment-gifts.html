Before you buy

  • Measure drawers and cabinets (width, depth and height).
  • Ask whether the walls are painted drywall; picture strips aren't for wallpaper.
  • Skip anything that needs drilling unless they own the place.

FAQ

Do Command strips damage walls?
Command sells them as damage-free when removed as directed. The listing says to clean the wall with rubbing alcohol first and not to use them on wallpaper.
Is the Joseph Joseph Nest set microwave safe?
No. Joseph Joseph lists it as dishwasher safe, except the steel sieve, which should be hand-washed, and not microwave safe.
