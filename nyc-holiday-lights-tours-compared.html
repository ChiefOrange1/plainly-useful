NYC Holiday Lights Tours Compared: Dyker Heights Bus Tour vs. Walking vs. DIY (2026)

New York's two headline holiday-light experiences are the Rockefeller Center tree in Midtown and the residential displays in Dyker Heights, Brooklyn. They are far apart, so the main decision is whether to see them on your own or book a guided tour that handles transport.

Key 2026 dates (from official sources)

  • Rockefeller Center tree: arrives Nov 7; lighting ceremony Wednesday, Dec 2, 7–10 p.m.; on view through mid-January 2027 (Rockefeller Center).
  • Dyker Heights: NYC Tourism lists an annual guided bus tour of the neighborhood and says more than 100,000 people visit each year. Individual homes set their own schedules, so there isn't an official start date.

Dates can change; confirm on the official pages linked in Sources before you go.

Practical tips

  • Weeknights are usually less crowded than weekends.
  • Dyker Heights is a residential neighborhood: stay on sidewalks and keep noise down.
  • Dress for standing still in the cold, not for walking.

FAQ

When is the 2026 Rockefeller Center tree lighting?
Rockefeller Center lists Wednesday, December 2, 2026, 7–10 p.m.
Do I need a tour to see Dyker Heights?
No. It's a public neighborhood, but tours handle transport and timing, which many visitors prefer on cold nights.
