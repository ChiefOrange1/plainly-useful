Kitchen Drawer Organization: Measure Once, Sort Once

Organization · Updated October 27, 2026

AI-assisted guide: researched and drafted with AI tools and fact-checked against the manufacturer and official pages listed under Sources. We have not hands-on tested these products or tours; nothing here is a personal review.

Most drawer organizers fail because they are the wrong size. Measure first, then decide what earns a spot in your most-used drawer.

Measure the inside, not the front

Measure interior width, depth and height. Note any lip or slide hardware that narrows the opening.

Expandable trays for odd sizes

Adjustable trays solve the 'almost fits' problem. Check both the minimum and maximum width.

The 'one drawer, one job' rule

Give each drawer a single job (cutlery, prep tools, wraps and bags, or the junk drawer) and move duplicates to a backup box. If something hasn't been touched by spring, it can probably go.

FAQ

How do I organize a kitchen drawer that's an odd size?
Use an expandable tray and check its minimum and maximum width against your interior measurement.
