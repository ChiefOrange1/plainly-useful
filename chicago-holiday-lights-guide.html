Chicago Holiday Lights 2026: ZooLights, Christkindlmarket and Tours Compared

Travel · Updated October 27, 2026

Specs and details come from the manufacturer and official pages listed under Sources. We have not hands-on tested these products or tours; nothing here is a personal review.

Chicago's holiday season has a few fixed anchors (Lincoln Park Zoo's ZooLights and the Christkindlmarket at Daley Plaza) plus guided tours that link downtown lights in one evening.

Official 2026 dates and hours

  • ZooLights (Lincoln Park Zoo): Nov 20, 2026–Jan 3, 2027, 4:30–9 p.m. Closed Tuesdays except Dec 22 and 29, and closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. Tickets are sold by the zoo (check the current price on its site), and it lists free admission on Nov 23 and 30 and Dec 7 and 14.
  • Christkindlmarket Chicago (Daley Plaza): Nov 20–Dec 24, 2026; Fri–Sat 11 a.m.–9 p.m., Sun–Thu 11 a.m.–8 p.m., with special hours on Thanksgiving and Christmas Eve. Choose Chicago lists admission as free, with additional markets in Wrigleyville and Aurora.

Prices and hours are quoted from the official pages accessed Oct 27, 2026; confirm before you go.

  1. Late afternoon: Christkindlmarket at Daley Plaza.
  2. Evening: a lights tour or a trip north to ZooLights.
  3. Check transit times and dress for wind off the lake.
