About Plainly Useful

Plainly Useful is an AI-assisted editorial site. Our guides are researched and drafted with the help of AI tools, then checked against the manufacturer, retailer or official tourism pages we cite. Each guide lists its sources and the date we accessed them.

What we do and don't do

  • We do not claim to have personally tested products or taken tours. We don't publish 'we tried it' or 'I love it' statements.
  • We do not invent reviews, star ratings, testimonials, discounts or prices. Prices change, so we don't list them; check the current price with the retailer.
  • We do summarize manufacturer-stated specs, care instructions and official event details, and explain how to choose.
  • We do earn money from some links. See the affiliate disclosure. Commissions never change what a product's maker says about it, and we only report those statements.

Who runs this site

Plainly Useful is run by a small independent publisher. Content production is largely automated with AI and reviewed against cited sources before publication.
