  • Compare the Amazon price with the maker's own site. Lodge's site showed the same price as Amazon on Oct 27, 2026.

FAQ

Enameled or bare cast iron for a gift?
Enameled needs no seasoning and handles acidic dishes and fridge storage, according to Lodge and Le Creuset. Bare cast iron costs less and suits high-heat bread baking and campfires, but needs drying and oiling.
What size Dutch oven should I give?
Le Creuset's rule of thumb is about one quart per serving, so 5 to 6 quarts suits most families.
