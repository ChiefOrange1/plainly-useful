Spice storage comes down to one question: where can you see every label? Choose the method that fits your space, then alphabetize or group by cuisine.

Keep it working

  • Write the purchase month on the lid.
  • Keep backup bags in one labeled bin elsewhere.
  • Group the jars you use most (salt, pepper, garlic, cumin, chili, cinnamon) nearest the stove.

FAQ

What is the best spice storage for a small kitchen?
Whichever lets you see every label in the space you have: a tiered pull-down rack for deep cabinets, a turntable for mixed jars, or a shallow drawer laid label-up.
